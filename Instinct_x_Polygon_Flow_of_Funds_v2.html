Flow of Funds

Follow $2,000 from a user’s bank account into an Instinct agent wallet on Polygon — earning while idle, spent on instruction, split between agents, and reconciled back. The glowing packet is the value; the camera rides just behind it.

  • ▶
    Press Start and the journey plays on its own, stop by stop.
  • ≡
    The banner up top explains each step — what happens, who's involved, and how long it takes.
  • ⏸
    Use the controls at the bottom to pause, step back or forward, restart, or change speed.
  • ✓
    At the end you'll see a full recap of every step.
